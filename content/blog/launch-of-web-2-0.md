Welcome to **Web 2.0**—the second iteration of my personal contribution to the Internet!

### Why "Web 2.0"?

If you remember version 1.0 of this site, it was an experiment in figuring out what kind of digital presence I actually wanted. Over time, it accumulated the usual modern web cliche: overly minimal design, boring layouts, and a lack of content (caused by the joyless experience of seeing the end product).

So for iteration 2, I decided to go in the exact opposite direction.

### Back to what i know

This new version is inspired by the mid-2000s personal web—the era of:
- Sharp window borders and 3D beveled panels (`outset` forever!).
- High-contrast color palettes and readable pixel layouts.
- Authentic 88x31 web badges, visitor counters, and "Under Construction" banners just where they should be.
- Fast, instant page loads with no multi-megabyte JavaScript frameworks required just to read a paragraph of text.

### The Engine Under the Hood

Even though the coat of paint is something you would see in Internet Explorer, the foundation is modern and reliable:
- **[Zola](https://www.getzola.org/)**: Super fast Rust-based static site generator.
- **Vanilla CSS**: Zero external CSS utility frameworks.
- **Nix Flakes**: Reproducible dev environment using one of my new favourite tools.

### What to Expect Next

Here is what will be arriving on this corner of the net in the coming weeks:
1. **Interactive Client-Side Toys & WASM Games**: The arcade window on the home page is currently running an old-school starfield screensaver, but it is primed for a custom WebAssembly mini-game engine.
2. **CTF & Security Writeups**: Notes, heap exploits, and post-mortems from recent capture-the-flag competitions, however this will be on the other website...
3. **Random Tech Musings**: Musings of the electromagnetic spectrum, Linux tinkering, and other obtuse software projects.
