📖 Pocket Pet Help & Controls

Primary Button Controls

  • Button A (Select): Cycle through the top and bottom care icons. In the heading minigame, guess LEFT.
  • Button B (Execute): Confirm selection / interact with active icon. In the heading minigame, guess RIGHT.
  • Button C (Cancel): Deselect icons, exit status meter, or cancel current prompt.
  • A + C Simultaneously: Toggle sound mute, or bring up reset dialogue.

Pet Care Icons

  • 🍽️ Feed: Feed a Meal (fills hunger heart) or Snack (boosts happiness, increases weight).
  • 💡 Lights: Turn lights off when your pet falls asleep at bedtime.
  • 🎮 Game: Heading game! Guess which direction the pet will turn. Win 3 of 5 rounds for maximum happiness!
  • 💉 Medicine: Cure sickness when the skull icon appears.
  • 🦆 Clean: Flush waste with the rubber ducky before sickness strikes.
  • ⚖️ Meter: Check Age, Weight, Hunger Hearts, and Happy Hearts.
  • 📣 Discipline: Train pet when it beeps for attention but its meters are full.
ℹ️ About Pocket_Pet.exe
Pocket_Pet.exe [Build 1997.10]
Authentic Gen-1 Virtual Pet Handheld Simulation

A zero-framework simulation inspired by 1996/1997 Gen-1 virtual keychain pets. Featuring a 32x19 dot-matrix LCD canvas, real-time biological clocks, offline starvation calculation, Web Audio 1-bit piezo buzzer synthesizer, and complete offline PWA service worker caching.

Persistence: Progress is synchronized with lachlan_pocket_pet_v1 in browser local storage and shared with Lachlan's World Desktop OS.
